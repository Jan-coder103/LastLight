# Dry Dock Crane — approved asset review

Draft ID: `dry-dock-crane` · Category: `landmark` · Status: **Owner approved for integration on 2026-09-29**
Source idea: `ASSET_IDEAS.txt` #33, "Port, mountain, and industrial areas"

## Purpose and intended placement

A shipyard gantry crane silhouette: two rail-mounted piers, a lattice girder over the portal, a
cantilevered boom, a slung counterweight, a machinery house, a trolley and cab, and a hook block
on two falls of cable. Intended as a harbour or shipyard district marker, or as the anchor of a dock
road.

## Dimensions and scale

- Declared `dimensions`: 15.0 × 23.4 × 25.1 m
- Measured (vertex-accurate): 15.00 × 23.40 × 25.05 m (variants 0/1), 18.03 m deep (variant 2)
- Girder deck at 17.2 m, apex at 23.2 m. **11 scout heights** — this is the tallest thing in the
  current candidate set, taller than the 16 m water tower.

## Pivot and front direction

Ground at y = 0. **z = 0 is the gantry rail centre** — the point a real gantry is positioned and
surveyed from. The boom cantilevers forward over the dock, so the asset is intentionally not
centred on its own bounds: it spans z −6.03 to +19.02.

**+Z is the direction the trolley and hook travel.** A placement's `rotationY` is the meaningful
control: it turns the whole dock frontage.

## Collider proposal

`center {0, 8.6, −0.4}`, `size {13.8, 17.2, 3.0}` — the two piers only.

The portal is 15 m wide and the boom is 17 m overhead, so the player can walk under the crane
between the piers. That is deliberate and it is the asset's best gameplay property: a gantry you
can drive under is a landmark you can use, not a wall. The cost is the accepted one from the fire
escape and the wind pump — a player can clip a pier leg, a sill or the hook block.

## Interaction points

| id           | label     | position    |
| ------------ | --------- | ----------- |
| `crane-pier` | Crane Pier | 0, 0, 2.4 |

Between the piers at deck level, clear of the collider. Reads as a control or maintenance point at
the base of a crane.

## Variants

| variant | boom | cab | hook drop |
| ------- | ---- | --- | --------- |
| 0       | full | level | 5.2 m |
| 1       | full | **tilted 0.12 rad** | 7.5 m |
| 2       | **sheared short** | level | 5.2 m |

Variant 2 is the strong one: the boom is cut back to 5 m of lattice with a torn stub at the break,
the counterweight and its hangers are gone, and the footprint drops from 25.1 m to 18.0 m. A
half-collapsed gantry is a different silhouette, not a recolour.

## Complexity

112 meshes / 1700 triangles (v0 and v1), 91 / 1448 (v2). 6 materials.

Lattice runs are four chords plus five zig-zag diagonals per bay, generated by one `addLattice`
helper that is called four times. That helper is the reason the structure reads as truss work for
a modest mesh count.

## What reads well

- **Far:** the A-frame king post and its two stay lines make an unmistakable triangle over a
  cantilevered arm. Nothing else in the world will produce that read.
- **Near:** the bogies and rail heads at the piers, the counterweight slung under the back reach,
  the cab and hook block, and the girder walkway rail.

## Rework after the first preview

Three real faults were visible in the first preview and are fixed:

- **The tie stays were aimed wrong.** A Three.js cylinder points along +Y; setting `rotation.x`
  *and* `rotation.z` with the default `XYZ` Euler order does not aim it at a target — it laid the
  stays almost horizontally. They are now placed with `atan2` on a single axis and run correctly
  from the apex to the boom tip and the back reach.
- **The rails overshot the piers** by 3 m at each end, so the crane appeared to stand on loose
  bars. Rail length is now tied to the pier inset.
- **The hazard band ran the full 14 m portal width** and read as a red stripe across the base. It
  is now two 1.6 m markers, one per pier.

## Unresolved questions

- Nothing moves. The trolley, hook and sheaves are frozen; there is no update hook in
  `createVisual` and animation is not allowed. This is the asset's main limitation and the reason
  it is a landmark rather than a set piece.
- The hook block hangs free with nothing on it. A slung container or a spreader frame would be a
  large readability win but would double the mesh count.
- The girder lattice is decorative rather than structural: the boom is a real cantilever shape but
  the chords are four independent bars with no connection detail at the joints.
- 15 m rail gauge is on the wide side. Check against whatever the world uses for dock track before
  this is placed next to anything.

## Validation performed

- `tsc --strict` typecheck clean, including `noUnusedLocals` and `noUnusedParameters`.
- All 3 variants built in Node with the project's three.js; vertex-accurate bounds measured and
  confirmed inside the declared `dimensions`; `minY` exactly 0.
- `createVisual` called twice per variant and compared mesh-for-mesh and triangle-for-triangle:
  identical. No unseeded randomness, no animation, no lights, no `NaN` transforms.
- **Previewed in headless Chromium with software WebGL** at the default three-quarter view. The
  misaimed stays, the overshooting rails and the full-width hazard band were all confirmed visible
  in the first build and confirmed gone in this one. Not viewed in the game engine.
